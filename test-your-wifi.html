Is It Your Wi‑Fi — Or Your Internet?

Run one free test with the WLAN Pros Toolbox app, paste the report below, and get an instant, color-coded read on where the problem is — then send it to BWDS for a plain-language answer from a real engineer.

How It Works

Four steps, about a minute

You'll use the free WLAN Pros Toolbox app — no account, no ads, nothing collected.

1

Get the free app

Install WLAN Pros Toolbox on the device with the Wi-Fi problem (links & QR codes below), or open the web version in your browser.

2

Run “Check My Connection”

It's the first button when the app opens. Stand where the Wi-Fi feels slow and tap it. The test takes about 30 seconds.

3

Tap “Copy”

When the result appears, tap the Copy button (top right). Your full report is now on the clipboard as plain text.

4

Paste it below and send

Scroll down, paste the report into the box, add your contact details, and send. We'll review it and get back to you with a plain-language answer.

iPhone/iPad tip: to include Wi-Fi signal details, tap “Capture Wi-Fi details” in the app when prompted (Apple restricts this data otherwise). Android captures it automatically. Either way, the report is still useful — send what you get.
