    About BWDS

    Beersheba Wireless Design Solutions

    Beersheba Wireless Design Solutions (BWDS), a NAD Network Services LLC company, specializes in tailored enterprise wireless network solutions. Our team brings over a decade of experience delivering reliable Wi-Fi surveys, predictive RF engineering, and secure wireless architecture for organizations that cannot afford network downtime.

    We work independently of any single hardware vendor — engineering the RF environment first, then deploying and validating on the platform that fits your environment, from Cisco Enterprise Networks to Ekahau and Hamina survey tooling to our own HiveRadar APoS gear.
