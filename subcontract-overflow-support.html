One Engagement, Your Bench

Available as a subcontract engagement

Bring us in for one phase of a project or the whole wireless scope — sized and priced per engagement, not a headcount commitment.

01

Ekahau Site Surveys

Active and passive RF surveys with Ekahau Sidekick 2, delivered as a white-labeled heatmap report under your cover page.

Use when: overflow site count or no in-house RF tech.

02

Predictive Wireless Design

AP placement and channel planning modeled against architectural floor plans — ready before drywall or an RFP deadline.

Use when: bidding a job that needs a real design, fast.

03

Post-Deployment Validation

Independent as-built validation confirming coverage and roaming meet spec before your team signs off to the customer.

Use when: closing out a punch list or a warranty claim.

04

AP Deployment Support

Mounting guidance, cable-drop coordination, and configuration support so your install crew isn't guessing at final placement.

Use when: your crew handles install, not RF.

05

Wireless Troubleshooting

Root-cause RF diagnostics for dead zones, roaming failures, or interference — brought in as a second set of eyes on an open ticket.

Use when: an escalation has stalled internally.

06

Cabling & GC Coordination

Cable-drop planning coordinated directly with electricians and general contractors so AP locations get cabled correctly the first time.

Use when: RF and construction schedules need to sync.

How It Works

Four steps from scope call to handoff

  1. 1

    Scope call

    Tell us site count, square footage, vertical, and timeline. We size the engagement in one call.

  2. 2

    NDA & fixed-fee quote

    Mutual NDA signed, scope and price confirmed in writing — typically within 24 hours.

  3. 3

    Field engagement

    Our engineers execute the survey, design, or deployment support work on your schedule, on-site or remote as scoped.

  4. 4

    White-label handoff

    You receive a client-ready deliverable under your branding. You present it — we stay behind the curtain.
